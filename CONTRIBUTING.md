# Contributing to mvd

Thank you for helping. Before you send a change:

- Read the README and the development doc for the part you're changing
  (docs/SERVER_DEVELOPMENT.md). The Apple apps are
  [mvd-apple](https://github.com/potato-video/mvd-apple), with their own.
- Run `make test`.
- Keep the docs current in the same change.

## Licensing your contribution

mvd is under the MIT License (see [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)). By sending a
contribution (a pull request, a patch, or anything else meant for
inclusion), you agree that it's licensed under the MIT License too, and
that it's your own work or you otherwise have the right to license it.

## The name

"mvd" and its icon identify Warren Merrifield's project. Forks are welcome,
but must use a different name and icon, and mustn't suggest
they're mvd or endorsed by it. Saying a fork is "based on mvd" is fine.
